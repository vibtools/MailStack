Privacy notice

How information submitted through the MailStack public website is handled.

Effective: 27 June 2026

1. Scope

This notice applies to information submitted through the public website at __PUBLIC_HOSTNAME__, including mailbox service inquiries. It does not replace any separate operational policy applicable to an activated MailStack account.

2. Information we collect

When you submit the contact form, we may receive your name, email address, company or organization, phone number, requested service, expected team size and the message you provide. Basic technical information may also be recorded for security and anti-spam purposes.

3. How information is used

Submitted information is used to review your request, respond to your inquiry, plan a suitable mailbox setup, protect the website from misuse and maintain an operational record of the request.

4. Sharing

Contact information is not sold. It is handled by the MailStack administration team and by infrastructure required to transmit and store the inquiry.

5. Retention

Inquiry records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for service follow-up, security review and operational recordkeeping.

6. Security

MailStack uses technical and administrative controls designed to protect contact submissions. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices

You may choose not to submit the form. Information marked as required is necessary for MailStack to review and respond to the request.

8. Contact

Questions concerning this notice may be submitted through the MailStack contact form.
